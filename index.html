I'm a founder and technologist who makes startups work: product, revenue, operations, and everything in between. I've built across music, art, and the wider internet economy.

Before this I led product at Zora, where creators earned more than a million dollars, and at Gitcoin. Earlier: music ventures at BCG Digital Ventures, music software at Ableton, monetization at SoundCloud. I've taught funding mechanisms at NYU Berlin, mentored founders at Gaza Sky Geeks, and spoken at Funding the Commons, ETHBerlin, Art Basel, and Amsterdam Dance Event.

I make things on the side. Marginalia, a private outfit diary for developing your own sense of style. closecontact.club, a privacy-first check-in app that ran across a dozen venues during the pandemic.
